# Daily Hisab 🥛

A simple tracker for everyday household services: milk, newspaper, maid, tiffin, water can and vegetables. Mark the days something didn't come, and Daily Hisab works out the monthly bill for you, so there are no more month-end arguments.

> **This is an early test version (prototype).** It runs in your phone's browser. An Android app is being built next. Your feedback will shape it.

## What it does

- **Track many services:** milk, newspaper, maid, tiffin, water can, vegetables, or anything else. Each has its own unit, daily quantity and rate.
- **Counts automatically:** the daily quantity is counted every day. Tap **Not came** only on days it didn't come.
- **Change quantity for a day:** use + and − for extra or less on any day.
- **Calendar view:** see the whole month at a glance. Skipped days show in red, and days with extra quantity are outlined.
- **Rate changes:** set a new rate from any date. Earlier days keep the old rate, so past bills stay correct.
- **Monthly bill:** totals per service and overall, with a Paid mark for each.
- **Share on WhatsApp:** send the monthly statement as a message.
- **Three languages:** English, हिन्दी and मराठी.
- **Works without login:** nothing to sign up for.
- **Backup and restore:** copy your data as text and paste it back later.

## How to use

1. Open the link and choose your language.
2. Go to **Setup** and tap **Add service**. Choose the type, the daily quantity, the rate and the start date.
3. Use the **Today** tab. Tap **Not came** on days a service was missed, or use + and − to change the quantity.
4. Open **Calendar** to review the month. Tap a date to edit that day.
5. Open **Bill** at the end of the month to see the totals, mark bills as paid, or share the statement on WhatsApp.
6. When a price goes up, go to **Setup**, tap the ✎ button on that service, and use **Change rate** with the date it starts.

## Your data and privacy

- Everything you enter is stored **only in your own browser, on your own phone**.
- No account, no server, no tracking, and nobody else can see your entries.
- If you clear your browser data, your entries will be erased. Use **Setup → Backup & restore → Copy** now and then, and keep the text somewhere safe (for example, in a WhatsApp chat with yourself).

## Known limitations of this test version

- No reminders or notifications yet
- No PDF statements (WhatsApp text only)
- The first load needs an internet connection
- Backup is copy-and-paste, not a file download
- Not yet tested on every phone and browser

## Run it yourself

Daily Hisab is a single file, `index.html`, with no build steps and no dependencies.

- **Locally:** download `index.html` and open it in a browser.
- **On GitHub Pages:** upload `index.html` to a public repository, then go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
